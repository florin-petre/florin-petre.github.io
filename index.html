How to play
  • Standard chess rules on files L, a–h, and R. Neutral Rift Wardens hold L4/L5 and R4/R5: they cannot move, be captured, or be crossed, and they do not attack. Click a piece, then a highlighted square.
  • Your outer pieces move as bishops, rooks, or knights to start.
  • Choose a different extra power for each rift piece: Knight leap, Rook stride, or Bishop glide. Rift pieces awaken after 3 moves; their chosen power is public and surges on moves 6, 9, 12, and so on.
  • Check, checkmate, castling, en passant, and pawn promotion are in play.
