✳ DUST & DAYBREAKA FRONTIER LIFE Back to Dry Creek ↗

A pocket guide for the newly arrived

Make a life out here.
One day at a time.

Dry Creek isn't much to look at from the train, but give it a day or two. Take your time: jobs run in real time, good gear is earned, and Ironjaw is not a beginner's problem.

01 — PUT IN A SHIFT

Work takes a minute

Take one job at a time. It runs for 60 seconds, even if you leave the tab or reload. Jobs favor different materials: delivery brings timber, ranch work brings hide, and saloon duty brings scrap and herbs. Material percentages are average units per job (120% means 1.2 on average); recipes show what each material is needed for.

02 — GROW YOUR TRADE

Level up & unlock

Every level adds another job to your daily limit. New work opens at levels 2–6. Recipes unlock on the workbench as you advance, and the Outfitters add better stock to match your level.

03 — BUILD YOUR KIT

Craft, refine & equip

The Inventory & craft tab shows your character, equipment, and stats. Fine gear needs finished materials: build an assay shed to smelt copper, or a tanning shed to cure hide, then use those stations at the workbench.

04 — FIND YOUR TRADE

Wear a set

Trailblazer gear balances every stat, Deadeye favors damage and aim, and Ironbark favors armor and dodge. Equip two pieces for the first bonus and four pieces for an additional bonus. Your character sheet tracks set progress.

05 — KEEP YOUR WORD

Daily errands

Complete three jobs, explore a trail, and finish the rotating side mission—gather materials, explore more trails, or win a gulch fight—to earn the daily bonus. Progress resets when you sleep, so unfinished errands forfeit that day's bonus.

06 — TAKE A CHANCE

Combat is part of the story

The sheriff's wanted-board chapter requires you to defeat both a Ridgeback coyote and a Gulch road-agent; they leave a trail mark and freight token that lead to Ironjaw after chapter 6, at level 3 and equipment power 18. Ironjaw always drops an ironheart. Build the Ironheart Forge upgrade onto your assay shed, temper 3 railsteel ingots, then craft and equip the Railbreaker rifle. Vane's chapter-10 showdown requires that rifle, level 8, and power 45. Aim improves hit chance, dodge may avoid a hit, and armor reduces damage.

07 — CLAIM YOUR PLACE

Build the homestead

Sign the Bell deed, buy planks, stone, and nails at the Builder's Yard, then build a cabin and choose your improvements. House expansions add plot space; gardens, woodlots, and pastures provide materials each morning. Comfort improves sleep, while watchtowers and palisades add combat stats.

08 — FOLLOW THE LEDGER

A solo frontier story

The Vane Ledger campaign follows Sardonix's family debt from a crumbling cabin to a railroad conspiracy. Ten chapters remember your jobs, trails, crafting, construction, and victories, and the final showdown unlocks in the last chapter. Take your time: unfinished chapter requirements persist between days.
