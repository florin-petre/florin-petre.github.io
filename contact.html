A pocket guide for the newly arrived

Make a life out here.
One day at a time.

Dry Creek isn't much to look at from the train, but give it a day or two. Take your time: jobs run in real time, good gear is earned, and Ironjaw is not a beginner's problem.

01 — PUT IN A SHIFT

Work takes a minute

Take one job at a time. It runs for 60 seconds, even if you leave the tab or reload. Jobs favor different materials: delivery brings timber, ranch work brings hide, and saloon duty brings scrap and herbs. Material percentages are average units per job (120% means 1.2 on average); recipes show what each material is needed for.

02 — GROW YOUR TRADE

Level up & unlock

Every level adds another job to your daily limit. New work opens at levels 2–6. Recipes unlock on the workbench as you advance, and the Outfitters add better stock to match your level.

03 — BUILD YOUR KIT

Craft, shop & equip

The Inventory & craft tab shows your character, every equipment slot, and your current stats. Weapons have a damage range. Gear can add damage, armor, aim, and dodge; those stats are used in combat.

04 — FIND YOUR TRADE

Wear a set

Trailblazer gear balances every stat, Deadeye favors damage and aim, and Ironbark favors armor and dodge. Equip two pieces for the first bonus and four pieces for an additional bonus. Your character sheet tracks set progress.

05 — KEEP YOUR WORD

Daily errands

Complete three jobs, explore a trail, and finish the rotating side mission—gather materials, explore more trails, or win a gulch fight—to earn the daily bonus. Progress resets when you sleep, so unfinished errands forfeit that day's bonus.

06 — TAKE A CHANCE

Enter the gulch

Choose a creature in Combat, then trade blows or retreat. Aim improves hit chance, dodge can avoid a whole attack, and armor reduces damage. Ironjaw is locked until level 3 and equipment power 18. Sleeping is free, restores energy and health, and is unavailable during jobs or fights.
