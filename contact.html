A pocket guide for the newly arrived

Make a life out here.
One day at a time.

Dry Creek isn't much to look at from the train, but give it a day or two. The folks are decent, the coffee is strong, and the trails have a habit of surprising you.

01 — FIND YOUR FEET

Take a job

The board has three jobs a day. Each one pays in dollars and experience, and some come with a chance to find something along the way. Keep an eye on your energy before you say yes.

02 — SEE WHAT'S OUT THERE

Pick a trail

Choose between the creek, the mesa, or the pines. Exploring costs energy but can turn up pocket money, useful odds and ends, or the occasional rough patch.

03 — MIND YOURSELF

Rest and recover

Stop at the Lantern for coffee to get some energy back, or call it a day to recover and see a fresh set of jobs at sunrise. Trail biscuits in your satchel are handy, too.

04 — GET TO KNOW THE PLACE

Make your mark

Experience brings new levels and a little extra grit. Finish the town's three daily jobs to earn the clerk's bonus. There's no hurry—this patch of frontier isn't going anywhere.
