A pocket guide for the newly arrived

Make a life out here.
One day at a time.

Dry Creek isn't much to look at from the train, but give it a day or two. Take your time: jobs run in real time, good gear is earned, and Ironjaw is not a beginner's problem.

01 — PUT IN A SHIFT

Work takes a minute

Take one job at a time. It runs for 60 seconds, even if you leave the tab or reload. Each job costs energy and pays out when the timer ends. Materials are common, useful items less so, and fine gear is a rare find.

02 — BUILD YOUR KIT

Gather & craft

Open Inventory & craft to see your materials, equipment, and recipes. Craft a revolver or redhide vest at the workbench, then equip it from your satchel. A tonic restores health during a fight.

03 — TAKE A CHANCE

Enter the gulch

Choose a creature in Combat, then strike until it falls or retreat. Weapon power increases your damage; coat power reduces incoming hits. A loss can be costly, so bring tonics and watch your health.

04 — EARN A LEGEND

Face Ironjaw

The boss is locked until you reach level 3 and equipment power 18. Finish jobs, complete the ledger's longer missions, and hunt for a charm before you pick that fight.
